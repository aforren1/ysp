ysp web demos

Stimulus gallery

Browser timing is not rig timing, and browser color is not calibrated color. The browser composites this canvas and may convert its colors for the display. The gallery shows what ysp/gfx.h draws, not luminance on this screen. How the web backend works.

Eight pages of tiles, one feature per tile: gratings, Gabors, noise and dots; shapes and strokes; paint and color spaces; images, masks and render targets; instances; curve text; the output stage. Some tiles move on a ysp/timeline.h time base.

The program is examples/gfx/gallery.c, compiled for the browser. Its canvas is 1200 x 760; on a smaller screen the page scales it down to fit.

Tap the right half of the screen, or press the Right arrow, Down arrow or Space, for the next page. Tap the left half, or press the Left arrow or Up arrow, for the previous page. 1 to 8: that page. Stop, or Shift+Esc on a keyboard, ends it.
