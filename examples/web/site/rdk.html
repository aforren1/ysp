Dot motion: a coherence threshold with a staircase

Browser timing is not rig timing. The times on this page are what the browser reports. The browser does not report when the display showed a frame, so the onsets are estimates. The threshold does not depend on them, but the dot speed and size are in pixels of this screen, not in degrees. How the web backend works.

Instructions

Each trial shows a cross, then a cloud of moving dots. Some of the dots move together to the left or to the right; the rest move at random.

Press the Left arrow or the Right arrow for the direction of the dots that move together. Keep your eyes on the cross. The cross turns green when you are correct and red when you are not.

The task gets harder after correct answers and easier after errors (a 2-down 1-up staircase). It ends after 8 reversals or 40 answered trials (60 in all), about 2 minutes. Shift+Esc or the Stop button ends the run early.
